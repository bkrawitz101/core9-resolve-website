What If the Work Could Give You Energy Instead of Taking It Away?

You have the potential to discover what brings your abilities alive, see hidden connections, restore movement in difficult situations, and turn possibilities into reality. Function 7 is the energy of realization. It awakens when your interests, talents, and abilities find meaningful expression. Taking care of yourself, work, relationships, travel, dancing, learning, and creative ventures can become sources of vitality rather than obligations. When this function is fulfilled, curiosity, movement, inspiration, and purpose create momentum. Its strength is not rescuing others: it is seeing what remains viable, what needs to change, and how life can move again.
