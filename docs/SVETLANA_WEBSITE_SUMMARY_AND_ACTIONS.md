### What's Incomplete or Disconnected
1. **Booking a Discovery Session doesn't go anywhere yet:** The *"Book a Discovery Session"* and *"Talk to a CORE9 Expert"* spots are just placeholder text until we have the real scheduling link.
2. **The 9 Function pages are dead ends:** Once a visitor clicks into any of the 9 Functions, there is no top menu, *"Back to Home"*, or *"Next Function"* button—they have to hit their browser's Back button to get out. (The *"Continue to Function 2"* button on Function 1 is currently turned off.)
3. **The 3 Assessments don't send people to book a session:** After someone finishes a test and sees their results, there is no link to book a Discovery Session or return to the main Home page. Also, the original Russian 9-question page has one broken link pointing to the 27-question test.

5. **Needs a real phone & computer walk-through:** Nobody has tested the entire journey end-to-end on real phones and desktop browsers yet (including every test result, language switch, and mobile screen layout).
