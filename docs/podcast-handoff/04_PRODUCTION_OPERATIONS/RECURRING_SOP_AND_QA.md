# Producer operating procedure — minimal founder time

## Editorial pipeline and statuses
**Idea → Source checked → Script draft → Comedy edit → Methodology check → Voice generation → Audio edit → QA approved → Scheduled → Published → Email sent → Analytics reviewed.** Each status timestamped, with owner and version. Do not bypass methodology or audio QA.

## Production buffer
Before launch, produce and approve 4 episodes per show (8 total) where practical. Maintain **4 weeks of ready-to-publish audio** (8 episodes total) and **at least 10 unused topics per show**. If queue falls below threshold, auto-draft topic suggestions for producer review; never auto-publish unreviewed AI audio.

## Weekly operating schedule (suggested; adjust release days after approval)
**Monday:** confirm Tuesday business episode is published/scheduled; check player, transcript, link, booking, RSS, email. Prepare Friday life episode.
**Tuesday:** publish Business Unusual; send business email after live check; verify delivery and links.
**Wednesday:** batch draft/record/edit future episodes; verify approved CORE9 excerpt; maintain buffer; update metadata and transcripts.
**Thursday:** preflight Friday life episode; check next week's two episodes and newsletter schedules.
**Friday:** publish Mr. Right & Mrs. Maybe; send life email after live check; verify delivery and links.
**Monthly:** audit top episode completion, page-to-booking clicks, subscribers, topic fatigue, audio quality, directory status; generate 10+ replacement ideas per show and approve next batch.

## Source fidelity and safety gate
For each script, record: approved source filename, section/page/heading, excerpt used, exact claim made in script, verifier and verification date. If no verified source supports a claim, remove it or label it as fiction/opinion; never manufacture CORE9 terms or therapeutic/business guarantees. Avoid diagnosis, investment advice, invented client case studies and false expert credentials.

## Episode packaging checklist
- Script with unique version and speaker labels
- Verified CORE9 source citation internal only
- Final MP3 plus lossless master, loudness checked and clipped-audio scan
- Unique show/episode number and stable slug
- Episode title, summary, teaser, tags, exact transcript, duration, cover image
- Podcast host episode ID and RSS publication status
- CORE9 episode page with tested audio player, links and CTA
- Email campaign with correct URL, tested unsubscribe and scheduled send
- Approval record and published timestamp

## Audio QA rubric (score 1–5)
Instant hook, believable characters, conflict escalation, laugh density, empathy, originality, voice consistency, intelligibility, pacing, source accuracy, satisfying ending, actual duration. **Release gate:** no score below 3, source accuracy 5/5, technical audio pass, producer signoff. Flag significant quality decline for founder review.

## Error handling
- Audio file missing: do not publish page or send email; notify producer.
- Email sent but page broken: immediately repair or redirect; record incident.
- Podcast directory delayed: CORE9 site launch continues; show badges only for live directories.
- RSS rejects episode: fix host metadata; do not change GUID; retry and validate.
- AI voice drifts: pause batch and restore approved voice preset; replace faulty export.
- Methodology error: unpublish or correct audio and transcript; document correction; notify founder if substantive.

## Governance
Owner retains control of domain, podcast host, email list and booking accounts. Give producer/developer least-privilege access; enable MFA; document revocation. No passwords or private keys in ZIP. All voice assets and music must have appropriate commercial licenses. Disclose fictional/AI-produced nature clearly on website and where required by distribution platform rules.

## Success metrics
- Per episode: page visits, play starts, listens/completions if player supports, average listening time, share/cross-show clicks, newsletter clicks, consultation CTA clicks and completed bookings if trackable with consent.
- Per show: follows/subscribers, unique listeners, completion, return visits, directory discovery.
- Monthly: which episode themes attract meaningful engagement, whether email visits explore archive, and whether consultation traffic converts.
- Privacy: use aggregated analytics and consent controls; never claim cross-platform tracking accuracy that the stack cannot deliver.
