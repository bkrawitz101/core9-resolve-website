## Read this first

Build and run **two fictional audio comedy shows**, **one episode of each show per week**, each approximately five minutes. The central conversion path is **weekly email → individual episode page on CORE9 website → explore both shows and the full archive → Seriously Curious? consultation CTA → real booking page**. Public podcast directories extend discovery; the CORE9 website remains the primary destination for emails and calls to action.

1. Open `01_START_HERE/EXECUTIVE_PRODUCTION_BRIEF.md` and `05_DEVELOPER_BUILD/DEVELOPER_BACKLOG.csv`.
2. Use `02_CREATIVE_AND_CONTENT/EPISODE_SLATE_40.csv` as the initial 20-week production queue; all titles are proposed creative editorial topics, **not approved source text**.
3. Read `02_CREATIVE_AND_CONTENT/SHOW_BIBLE_AND_MASTER_PROMPTS.md` before generating any episode.
4. Implement the page, player, archive, email, tracking and booking as described in `03_WEBSITE_AND_COPY/WEBSITE_CONTENT_AND_UX.md` and `05_DEVELOPER_BUILD/TECHNICAL_SPEC_AND_ACCEPTANCE.md`.
5. Follow the launch and weekly checklists. **No unattended public AI publishing**: require producer QA, with founder review only for initial pilots and significant changes.

## Critical constraints
- **Two episodes TOTAL per week**: 1 Business Unusual + 1 Mr. Right & Mrs. Maybe; not two episodes per show.
- **Business Unusual** is primarily for business owners and founders. **Mr. Right & Mrs. Maybe** is for everyday life, relationships, friendships, couples, employees, employed managers and partnerships. Do not assume everyone owns a business.
- Both shows: fast, conflict-based, funny, empathetic, human, non-stereotyped. Entertainment first; insight second; consultation invitation third.
- Use only verified approved CORE9 methodology; fictional stories and guest characters are permitted if labeled as fiction.
- Source documents are included as a separate unchanged ZIP. A source index may be outdated; prefer the latest authoritative source and correction when conflicts arise.
- No live booking URL, brand domain, newsletter provider, host credentials or production voices were supplied. Developer must obtain these from owner; **do not invent links or claim deployment**.
- Proposed weekday releases: Tuesday Business, Friday Life; weekdays are suggestions requiring scheduling confirmation.
- Proposed consultation CTA: `Seriously Curious?` with button `Talk to a CORE9 Expert`; exact page copy included, to be approved for publication.
